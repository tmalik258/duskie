# Where things are in Settings

## Appearance

Island colour, mascot colour and themes: Settings > Appearance.

## General

Starting with Windows, your day and Today, History with Undo, and Backup: Settings > General.

## Sounds

UI sounds, volume and the sound kit: Settings > Sounds.

## Notifications

Do Not Disturb, Focus, how loud a notification is, and what Duskie learned about your senders: Settings > Notifications.

## AI models

Which AI answers in Ask and which agent writes code, API keys, the local model, the vision model and SemIf: Settings > AI models.

## Voice

Wake word, speak answers, interrupt, conversation (reply without the wake word), how long Duskie waits for a reply, word fixes, the voice and its speed, Live history, and Teach Duskie your voice: Settings > Voice.

## Memory

What Duskie remembers about you, all editable: Settings > Memory.

## Skills and automations

Switch skills On, Off or Auto, automations, Morning setup, and the skills library with Sync now: Settings > Skills.

## Apps

Composio, Gmail, IMAP, calendars, Fathom, MCP servers, the browser extension (Install next to each browser), Claude Code hooks and Codex (Add for me): Settings > Apps.

## Code and Git

Where your projects live, the code editor, and Git: Settings > Code and Git.

## Shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the new keys: Settings > Shortcuts. Ask, Agents, Git, Talk, Accept, Not now, Ask about the screen, Clipboard history, Pause and Settings can all be recorded.

## Privacy

What Duskie notices, ignored apps and sites, search folders, pausing sensors, and Found on this PC: Settings > Privacy.

## Storage

What takes space on this PC: Settings > Storage.

## Updates and diagnostics

Updates show on the island when one is ready; hover it and pick Update. Copy diagnostics and Offer to report crashes are under Settings > Updates.
