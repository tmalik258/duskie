# Where things are in Settings

## Appearance

Island colour, mascot colour and themes: Settings > Appearance.

## General

Starting with Windows, quiet hours, Focus and Do Not Disturb: Settings > General.

## AI models

Which AI answers in Ask and which agent writes code, API keys, the local model and the vision model: Settings > AI models.

## Voice

Wake word, speak answers, interrupt, conversation (reply without the wake word), Live answers, word fixes, the voice and its speed, and Teach Duskie your voice: Settings > AI models > Voice.

## Memory

What Duskie remembers about you, all editable: Settings > Memory.

## Skills and automations

Switch skills On, Off or Auto, automations, Morning setup, and the skills library with Sync now: Settings > Skills and automations.

## Connections and apps

Composio, Gmail, IMAP, calendars, Fathom, MCP servers, the browser extension (Install next to each browser) and Claude Code hooks (Add for me): Settings > Connections and Settings > Apps.

## Shortcuts

Click a shortcut, then press the new keys: Settings > Shortcuts. Talk, Accept, Not now, Ask about the screen, Clipboard history, Pause and Settings can all be recorded.

## Privacy and data

What Duskie notices, ignored apps and sites, search folders, and pausing sensors: Settings > Privacy and data.

## Storage

What takes space on this PC: Settings > Storage.

## Updates and diagnostics

Updates show on the island when one is ready; hover it and pick Update. Copy diagnostics and Backup are under Settings > Home > About and Backup.
